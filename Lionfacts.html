Fun Facts About Lions

  • Pack of Lions?: Unlike other big cats, are very social. They live in groups called Prides and roam the vegetation together, with the most dominant male leading the group.
  • Powerful Voice: A lion's roar can be heard up to thirty feet away!
  • Rare hybrids: A crossbreed between a male lion and a female tiger is called a Liger, and a cross breed between a female lion and a male tiger is called a Tigon!
